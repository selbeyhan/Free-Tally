# Privacy Policy for Free Tally

**Last updated: September 2026**

Free Tally does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data,
whatsoever.

- No account or sign-in of any kind.
- No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.
- No network access — the app makes no network requests at all.
- No data ever leaves your device.

All your counters and settings are stored only in the app's local storage on
your own iPhone or iPad, using standard iOS on-device storage (a local file
and `UserDefaults`). Nothing is backed up to any server operated by the app
or its developer. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

The app requests no permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts,
photos, or notifications).

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach out via
the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/selbeyhan/Free-Tally).
